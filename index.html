THE PROCESS

What students should keep track of.

Admissions happen in stages. Missing a counselling deadline can affect seat consideration even after a good entrance-exam result.

01

Eligibility & documents

Check Class 12 subject requirements, minimum marks, age rules (if any), category and domicile conditions. Keep certificates and identity details consistent.

02

Exam registration

Read the information bulletin before applying. Check photo/signature specifications, fee payment, correction windows, confirmation page and application number.

03

Exam & result

Track city intimation, admit card, exam date, answer key, objection window, scorecard and rank/percentile publication separately.

04

Counselling & choice filling

Register separately for JoSAA, CSAB, state counselling and private-university counselling where applicable. Compare opening/closing ranks and seat categories.

05

Seat acceptance

Follow each round’s seat-acceptance fee, document verification, willingness/float-freeze-slide rules and reporting instructions. Read the current business rules.

06

Final admission

Confirm institute fee payment, original-document requirements, medical or anti-ragging forms, hostel process and reporting date directly with the allotted college.

KEEP THESE READY

Common document checklist

Exact requirements differ by exam, counselling body, category and institute.

  • Class 10 certificate / date-of-birth proof
  • Class 12 marksheet or appearing-student details
  • Recent photograph and signature
  • Government photo identity document
  • JEE / entrance application and scorecard
  • Category, EWS, PwD or domicile certificates, if applicable
  • Seat allotment letter and fee receipts
